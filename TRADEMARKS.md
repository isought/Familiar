## Brand assets

The brand assets are:

- the project's name and logo;
- the app icon: `Resources/AppIcon.icns` and any rendering of it;
- the sticky-note character: its look and expressions, and any rendering of it. This covers renderings produced by the code in `Sources/Familiar/Features/Companion/` as well as drawings of it anywhere else.

From this version on, `Resources/AppIcon.icns` is not licensed under the Apache License: © 2026 Yang Lu, all rights reserved. Earlier releases stay under the terms they were published with. The character's drawing code remains Apache-2.0 code; this policy is about using the character as a brand.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is based on, compatible with, or a fork of this project.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases, with their name and icon intact.
- Write about the project, show screenshots, and teach people how to use it.

## What needs written permission

- Using the name, icon or character as, or as part of, the name, icon or mascot of your own app, fork, service, company or domain.
- Distributing modified builds under the project's name or icon. Before you distribute a fork, change its name, bundle identifier, icon and character.
- Anything that suggests the project or its maintainer endorses, supports or is affiliated with your product.

## Provenance

Yang Lu created the sticky-note character and the app icon for this project, working with AI tools:

1. On 2026-09-23, Yang Lu generated a concept image with ChatGPT (OpenAI).
2. The character was redrawn in SwiftUI code (`Sources/Familiar/Features/Companion/Mascot.swift`) and refined through Yang Lu's design reviews.
3. The app icon is rendered from that code.

The concept image is not distributed with the project.
