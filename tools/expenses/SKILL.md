Fictional example for a made-up company at example.com: the policies and data below are invented. Replace them with your company's own.
The expense tool is where employees file expense reports. Flow: employee submits → manager approves → finance pays.
Receipts are required on any line over $25. Meals are capped at $75 per person per day.
Approvals normally take up to 3 business days; after that the employee should nudge the approver.
