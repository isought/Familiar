# Expense reports (Concur)

Internal URL: https://expenses.internal.example.com

## Submitting an expense
1. Click **New Report** in the top right.
2. Report name format: `YYYY-MM <purpose>` e.g. `2026-09 Client dinner`.
3. Attach a receipt for every line over $25. Photos of receipts are fine.
4. Pick a **Cost Center**. If yours is missing, ask your manager for the code; do not pick "General".
5. Click **Submit**. Your manager approves within 3 business days.

## Common problems
- **"Submit" button is greyed out**: a required field is empty. Usually Cost Center or a missing receipt on a line over $25. Scroll up, red text marks the line.
- **"Policy violation: meal limit"**: meals are capped at $75 per person per day. Split the receipt across attendees or add a justification in the Comments box.
- **Report stuck in "Pending approval"** for more than 3 days: ping your manager, then finance-help@example.com.
