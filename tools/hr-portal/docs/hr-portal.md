# HR portal (Workday)

URL: https://hr.internal.example.com (VPN required)

## Time off
- **Menu → Time Off → Request Time Off**. Select days on the calendar, then click **Submit** at the bottom.
- Balance shows in the top right. It updates the day after your request is approved.
- Sick days do not need approval but still must be entered.

## Pay slips
- **Menu → Pay → Payslips**. Click a date to download the PDF.

## Changing personal details
- **Profile (top right) → Personal → Edit**. Address changes take effect on the next pay run.
- Bank details can only be changed from the office network or VPN, and require a second confirmation email.

## Common errors
- "Your session has expired": Workday logs out after 20 minutes idle. Reload the page and sign in again; drafts are saved.
- "You do not have permission to view this page": you are probably not on VPN. Connect and reload.
