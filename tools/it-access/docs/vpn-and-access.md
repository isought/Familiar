# VPN and access requests

## VPN
- Use **GlobalProtect** from the Dock. Portal address: `vpn.example.com`.
- If it says "Could not connect to gateway": quit GlobalProtect fully (menu bar icon → Quit), reopen, and sign in again. If it still fails, restart your Mac before calling IT.
- VPN is required for: HR portal, finance systems, internal wiki. It is NOT required for email or Slack.

## Requesting access to an internal system
1. Open the **Access Hub** at https://access.internal.example.com.
2. Search for the system by name (e.g. "Salesforce", "Jira").
3. Click **Request**, choose a role, and give a one-line business reason.
4. Your manager approves, then the system owner. Typical time: 1-2 business days.
5. You get an email when it's done. Log out and back in to the system to see the new role.
